Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting and maintaining your locs

Can I start my locs with relaxed hair?

Yes you can always start your locs with either natural or relaxed hair.

For how long can I go without retie maintenance?

First retie has to be after 6 weeks after which you can do 6-8 weeks. That's approximately 6-8 times yearly.

How many inches of hair can I start my locs with?

You can always start your locs with as short as 3 inches of hair, however, not all methods of installation is suitable for such short length.

What's the difference between the 3 locs methods?

All methods gives the same end result with proper retie maintenance, however, installation method is determined by your hair type, density and length.

When do my locs mature?

Locs maturity depends on the method you start with. Some method takes longer while other takes shorter. A proper consultation would give you adequate information on what you should expect.

Why are my locs unravelling?

Your locs early stage are prone to unravelling. Few at the back might come off because your back hair are always softer than the front and middle which makes it more prone to unravelling.

Which method is the best to start your locs?

There is no good or bad method to start your locs. It depends on your personal preferences, how you expect your locs to look in the future, your hair length, density and type.
